# Global Intervention Coalition (GIC)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

**Global Intervention Coalition (GIC)** is a global military organization working directly with [[VISTA]]. It was established by VISTA delegation vote on 4 December 2008, with [[Noradus|Northrad]] as its founder and architect, following the 2008 raids and the earlier [[Ironish Task Force (ITF)|ITF]]. It is a separate institution from ITF: ITF is the military that can function independently on its own, while the GIC is a different body. Most people call the GIC "the global ITF" because of ITF's massive influence; the confusing naming is intentional. The GIC established [[Anajak Forces]].

## Institutional posture

The GIC is recorded as the loudest institutional voice pressing VISTA to act against [[Konish's Group]]. Its position is that its mandate, established on 4 December 2008, is narrower than the threat the [[2008 Pocheasang Palace raid]] demonstrated, and that the charter's restrictions on offensive operations leave the coalition unable to respond.

The pressure is directed at the twelve delegations, seeking amendment of the charter and authorisation of operations into the [[Ironclad DMZ]]. The delegations have not acted on it. No amendment and no offensive campaign are recorded.

The GIC has therefore become the institution created to work around a deadlock, pressing the deadlocked body to authorize the work it was built for.

## The mirrored answer

The GIC is the institutional answer to [[Konish's Group]]. Both are umbrella coalitions: one of generals and specialised forces, the other of autonomous Cladish militias. Beneath them, the answer is repeated a level down: [[Anajak Forces]] answers [[LENDERMAIRE]], the task force meeting the black operations unit. See [[Anajak Forces]].
