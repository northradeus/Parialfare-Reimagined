# Maire O'Mare



**Maire O'Mare** is an insurgent group in [Ironclad](/km/locations/countries/ironclad), active in 2008. It presents itself as the modern return of the ideology of the medieval Maire O'Mare movement led by Rogolf.

It is not the person returning. It is the idea.

## 1. The return

The medieval movement sought independence for the Cladish from Ironish authority, and was publicly mistaken for a nationalist restoration of the Deck Empire. That misreading persists.

The 2008 insurgency may therefore be claiming either the medieval movement itself or the misreading of it, and the record does not establish which.

In 2008 the ideology has been adapted to modern warfare. The faction is recorded as chaotic. It sometimes takes the side of [Konish Kimbotin](/characters/lendermaire/konish-kimbotin-(reimagined)), and frequently turns against him. It wants control of part of the region until Konish steps in.

## 2. The attention

The insurgency draws particular attention from [Ironland](/locations/countries/ironland), [Lurkinsberg](/km/locations/countries/lurkinsberg), and [Thaiphon](/locations/countries/thaiphon). All three were members of the coalition that broke the Deck Empire, and Ironland was founded from the faction that betrayed that coalition.

Thaiphon's attention is recorded as the sharpest of the three. The record does not establish what Thaiphon gained from the Deck's fall, or what specifically it fears from a movement claiming to restore it. Its relationship with the Shijo is recorded elsewhere and is not addressed here.

## 3. Konish's view

Konish regards Maire O'Mare as extreme, and as a threat capable of outlasting his own coalition. He has prioritised its destruction over its absorption. He fears the figure and the name because of Rogolfenstine.

The distinction matters. Konish can absorb rival warlords, men who want land or money. He cannot absorb an ideology.

## Unresolved

- **Descent:** whether the claim of descent from the medieval movement is true, and whether the insurgency itself believes it.
- **Thaiphon:** what Thaiphon gained from the Deck's fall, and what it now fears from a movement claiming to restore it.
- **The pronoun:** the historical record sometimes refers to Maire O'Mare in the feminine, as a person or legend rather than a movement. Whether this reflects how the name is remembered in-world, or is a quirk of the record, is unresolved.
- **Targets:** whether it fights VISTA, the warlords, or both is not established.
- **ITF losses:** whether the [ITF](/factions/ironish-task-force-(itf)) has ever lost soldiers to it is not recorded.
