# LENDERMAIRE



<p class="lead">**LENDERMAIRE** (native name: *Lendus O Mairen*) is a clandestine Cladish special operations formation active in the [[Ironclad DMZ|Ironclad]] region, operating as a black operations unit within the umbrella coalition [[Konish's Group]]. Founded in 2007 and garrisoned in southern Ironclad, the group maintains a core roster of thirteen members in addition to [[Konish Kimbotin (Reimagined)|Konish Kimbotin]], who led it operationally. Its motto is *"Let them taste steel."* It fields a modern military arsenal.</p>

The group remained unknown in open sources until the [[2008 Pocheasang Palace raid]], after which its name entered public reporting through civilian identification of an emblem in recovered bodycam footage. The [[VISTA]] Intelligence Agency subsequently described it as one formation within Konish's Group, a coalition of armed formations operating with substantial autonomy rather than as a single command. VISTA could not establish Kimbotin's supreme command over the group, and the proposition remained contested.[^2]

## 1. Concealment and emergence

Before August 2008 no organisation using the name LENDERMAIRE appeared in open sources, and no claim of responsibility, communiqué, or media reference to the name has been located by researchers. The formation was subsequently characterised as combining a clandestine command with relationships of material support to criminal organisations operating inside Ai'ron, and as one component of the larger [[Konish's Group]].[^2]

Public disclosure followed the 2008 Pocheasang Palace raid. Footage from three body-worn cameras carried by the assailants was recovered by the [[Laopanster Gendarmerie]] and published by Ai'ron authorities on 3 September 2008; it showed an emblem on the assailants' equipment that had no prior reference in open sources. On 10 September 2008, individuals in Ai'ron's Cladish population independently identified the emblem, and the name LENDERMAIRE entered public reporting. On 16 September 2008 the VISTA Intelligence Agency reported that LENDERMAIRE was one formation within Konish's Group.[^2] *Main article: [[2008 Pocheasang Palace raid]]*.

## 2. Organization and command

Kimbotin served as the group's operational lead and is widely described as its commander, holding the title of Supreme Warlord. Within Konish's Group, LENDERMAIRE is assessed to have answered to him, though his overall authority over the wider coalition remains contested by intelligence analysts. The VISTA Intelligence Agency's 2008 report described Konish's Group as a coalition of armed formations operating with substantial autonomy rather than as a single command, and recorded that Kimbotin's position within it — including the proposition that he held supreme command — could not be established from the available material. That proposition was advanced principally in public discussion rather than in the agency's findings, and remained contested.[^2]

[[Hanktonio]] served as the group's second-in-command, leading it in Kimbotin's absence. The group's ideological basis is not established in public records.[^3]

## 4. Internal dynamics

### 4.1 The household

LENDERMAIRE does not quarter its members in conventional military barracks. The core membership, the background personnel, and Kimbotin share a single residence on land Kimbotin owns within the Ironclad DMZ, adjacent to the camps of the wider [[Konish's Group]] forces. The record does not describe the interior layout.

The household is recorded as volatile. Members argue, disrupt each other's routines, and overlap in function. The unresolved division of labour between [[Gunter]] and [[Grunt]] is one documented instance; the record does not indicate whether it originates in the domestic arrangement or in the field.

### 4.2 Camaraderie

Despite the volatility, the record documents sustained mutual care among the core membership. Members are described as regarding one another as family, a designation that includes the background personnel; [[Joey]] and [[Gazmo]] are not treated as separate from the household despite not being counted among the thirteen.

**The Unspoken Rule: Recognition Without Disclosure.** The household functions on a single social rule: nobody asks what happened to anybody. Every member has been asked, once, by an institution with a clipboard, and they know how it felt. They recognize what a cast-off looks like—the exit-checking, the fast eating, the bag that is never fully unpacked—and they do not ask about it. Recognition is not the same as trust. It is a truce. The house forgives nearly everything except being left out of the table.

**The Plate Echo.** Care is expressed through logistics. A covered plate is routinely left on the counter and taken upstairs to [[Samsara Lykor]], who does not eat at the table. New arrivals find a plate set in front of them without observing who placed it. The household feeds its strays by the same method, whether they sit at the table or not.

Three relationships are specifically recorded:

*   **[[Hanktonio]] and the unit.** Hanktonio is the primary de-escalator during internal disputes. His role is described in the record as keeping the group intact.
*   **[[Leepy]] and [[Vanny]].** Leepy, the most compromised member of the roster, relies on Vanny as his primary social anchor. This friendship is recorded across every variant and continuity.
*   **[[Kaliber]] and the unit.** Kaliber is mute. The unit maintains an established nonverbal system of communication with him. The record does not describe it.

### 4.3 Kimbotin's domestic role

Kimbotin's public image as a warlord and his recorded function within the household are distinct. Intelligence material describes him as a supreme commander and a strategic threat. Members describe him as a central figure responsible for their stability, a role that extends beyond operational command to the daily management of the residence.

His conditioning of [[Leepy]] operates continuously rather than only in the field. The record does not establish how much of the household's stability depends on his continuous presence, or what happens to it in his absence.

### 4.4 The concealed member

The thirteenth member, [[Samsara Lykor]], lives under the same roof as the rest of the household, but is recorded as an outsider to its social structure. He does not depend on the unit's cohesion mechanism and keeps his membership by personal choice. His presence in the household and his separation from it are both recorded facts; the record does not reconcile them.

### 4.5 Field and home

The volatility of the household does not appear in operational records. The 2008 Pocheasang raid, the ITF infiltration, and the corporate raids are described in terms of precision and control. The transition between the domestic arrangement and deployed operation is not documented.

### 4.6 Structural anomaly

LENDERMAIRE is structurally anomalous among the armed factions of the region. The [[Ironish Task Force (ITF)]] operates as a corporate entity with a contractual chain of command. [[Anajak Forces]] is an institutional task force assembled and graded by a facility pipeline. The [[Laopanster Gendarmerie]] is a national branch of the [[Ministry of the Interior]].

LENDERMAIRE is recorded as none of these. It maintains no formal rank structure beyond Kimbotin's command and Hanktonio's secondary authority, no standardized training pipeline, and no external oversight. Its members are selected for psychological deviation rather than standardized aptitude. The record establishes no other armed formation in the region organized as a shared household.

## 5. History

### 5.1 Formation

LENDERMAIRE was established in 2007 by Konish Kimbotin, who personally selected the group's core members. The unit was created to operate as a specialized black operations force within the broader [[Konish's Group]] coalition. *Main article: [[Formation of LENDERMAIRE]]*.

### 5.2 2008 Pocheasang Palace raid

On 25–26 August 2008, thirteen LENDERMAIRE participants and the operational lead, Kimbotin, entered the palace complex at [[Pocheasang]], the seat of government of the Kingdom of Ai'ron. The group designated the operation *Operation: Sparta*. The force engaged the [[Laopanster Gendarmerie]] and the contracted [[Ironish Task Force (ITF)]], resulting in twelve defending fatalities, before withdrawing with classified military deployment plans, weapons-research materials, and computer hard drives.[^1] Early wire reporting placed the assault at the adjoining postal administration annex, producing the misnomer *2008 Post-Office Attack*, corrected within 24 hours.[^5]

The raid was the first public disclosure of LENDERMAIRE, which had not previously appeared in open sources. Following identification of the group's emblem and of Kimbotin's distinctive coat by Cladish individuals in Ai'ron, the name entered public reporting on 10 September 2008. On 16 September 2008, the VISTA Intelligence Agency reported that LENDERMAIRE was one formation within Konish's Group.[^2] *Main article: [[2008 Pocheasang Palace raid]]*.

### 5.3 Post-2008 activities

Following the Pocheasang raid, LENDERMAIRE conducted several operations for which exact dates remain unestablished in open-source records:
*   **ITF Infiltration:** The group reportedly infiltrated the majority of [[Ironish Task Force (ITF)|ITF]] bases.
*   **Company Raids:** The group conducted raids on corporate entities, which intelligence assessments later determined inadvertently assisted ITF operations. This is the only recorded instance in which the unit is assessed to have operated outside direct command oversight.
*   **Riot Cover Raid:** A government building raid executed during a civilian riot. The operation was planned by Kimbotin, and the extracted information was passed to Konish's Group to support frontline operations.

## 6. Methods and operations

LENDERMAIRE operates both within and outside of target countries, and has been recorded moving in and out of Ironland. Its operational portfolio includes direct raids, extractions, and the collection of classified information. Assailants in the 2008 raid carried assault kits and battle-ready loadouts, including demolition charges and portable jamming equipment; no heavy weapons, vehicles, or aerial systems were recorded.[^2]

The thirteenth member is reported to operate alone, completing objectives left unfinished by the rest of the group.[^3]

## 7. Equipment and funding

The group fields a modern military arsenal, including Cladish-style assault rifles, submachine guns, ballistic armor plates, and nano-flash pellets. Its funding is attributed to [[Konish's Group]]. Reports indicate that Kimbotin finances operations by capturing and reusing equipment from the [[Ironish Task Force (ITF)]].[^4]

## 8. Relations

*   **[[Konish's Group]]:** The parent coalition. The VISTA Intelligence Agency described it in 2008 as a coalition of armed formations operating with substantial autonomy. It includes established militias that engage ITF on the frontline. LENDERMAIRE passes extracted information to this parent body.
*   **[[Ironish Task Force (ITF)]]:** ITF regards LENDERMAIRE as a primary threat following the death of nine of its personnel in the 2008 raid. The relationship between ITF and the former Ironish Special Forces members within LENDERMAIRE (Markless, Kaliber, and Andrew) remains unresolved.
*   **[[Laopanster Gendarmerie]]:** The Ai'ron paramilitary force that suffered three fatalities during the 2008 raid.
*   **[[Anajak Forces]]:** A task force founded by [[Noradus|Northrad]] specifically to combat LENDERMAIRE, other Cladish factions, and entities disrupting peace in Ironland and other VISTA countries.
*   **[[VISTA]]:** The intergovernmental organization possesses limited intelligence on Konish's forces. In public discourse Kimbotin is described as the most wanted man in the region; the characterisation is a public one rather than a formal designation by VISTA.

## 9. Disputed records

Open-source intelligence and [[VISTA]] reports contain several inconsistencies regarding LENDERMAIRE's roster and history:

- **Roster numbering:** Records conflict on whether Andrew and Lykor are the twelfth and thirteenth members. Earlier documents designated Leepy as "fourth" and Lykor as "thirteenth," creating discrepancies in the count.
- **2008 raid participation:** It is unknown whether the thirteenth member (Lykor) participated in the 2008 Pocheasang raid.
- **Role ambiguity:** Records do not clarify whether Gunter or Grunt is responsible for grenade deployment versus suppressive fire.
