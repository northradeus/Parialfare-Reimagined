# Alpha-01



**Alpha-01** is the [ITF](/factions/ironish-task-force-(itf)) label for the classified assassin core of [Anajak Forces](/km/factions/anajak-forces). The team's whole is named Anajak; Alpha-01 designates only its classified core, and is not a separate organisation.
