# Operation Fallow

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

**Operation Fallow** was an intelligence operation of the [[Global Intervention Coalition (GIC)]], conducted after the establishment of [[Anajak Forces]], with the stated objective of retrieving a Lurkish national designated "Northrad" from the Cladish household [[LENDERMAIRE]]. It proceeded over the objection of the commanding general of Anajak Forces and concluded with the asset's return on terms the institution had not anticipated.

The record does not establish the operation's date. It establishes only that it followed the establishment of Anajak Forces.

## 1. Intelligence basis

The operation originated in a documentary cross-reference rather than in field intelligence.

The [[Ironish Task Force (ITF)]] maintains a recruitment list of Lurkish military personnel assessed as suitable for contractor employment. The designation "Northrad" appears on that list as a former marine and SWAT officer with a record of exceptional service.

Separately, the GIC held records pertaining to a Pocheasang establishment with known ties to [[Konish's Group]]. Those records identified an individual by the same designation as having worked as security at the establishment before his disappearance from public record.

The two documents were compared. The individual on the ITF list and the individual at the club were assessed as the same person. Because the establishment's ownership had established links to Konish's Group, the assessment concluded that the individual had entered the household.

No source is recorded as having disclosed this information. The operation's premise rests on two existing documents being read together by an analyst.

## 2. The assessment

The GIC assessed the individual as a misplaced asset rather than a member of the household. The basis for this assessment is not recorded in detail. It is recorded that the individual's service record, his placement on the ITF recruitment list, and the absence of any recorded defection supported the conclusion that his presence in LENDERMAIRE was temporary, involuntary, or both.

The assessment did not account for the possibility that he had chosen to remain.

## 3. The asset

The operation was assigned to [[Tanishito]], an S-grade operative of Anajak Forces, facility-raised and trained for intelligence work as well as for the assassin role under which she is listed.

Her cover was constructed as follows: Lurkish nationality; a military background consistent with her actual training; a documented record of instability and debt sufficient to explain a search for work in a dangerous district; and placement as a bartender at the same establishment whose records had produced the original identification.

The record describes the cover as "built to be found" and does not elaborate.

## 4. The objective and the objection

The stated objective was contact and retrieval: to make contact with the asset and effect his return to ITF control. The operation's secondary instruction, in the event of non-compliance, is recorded as "effect contact and reassess." The record does not establish what reassessment would have entailed.

The operation carried no extraction window. The asset was to remain in place until contact was made and the objective resolved by other means.

General [[General Ramcher|Ramcher]], commanding Anajak Forces, objected. The record establishes that he required two answers before authorising an insertion: the origin of the report concerning the asset's partner, and a defined procedure for the event that the operative could not be reached. Neither answer is recorded as having been provided.

The operation proceeded over his objection. The record does not establish who authorised it, and does not describe the authority under which it was conducted.

## 5. The return

Contact was made. The asset returned to GIC control.

He did not return as a soldier. He agreed to join the GIC on a single condition: that he operate independently. The record lists the terms as no rank, no tasking, no chain of command, and no oversight. He cannot be ordered. He cannot be located reliably. He cannot be recalled.

The GIC accepted the terms. The record does not describe the deliberation, and does not establish whether the arrangement was assessed as temporary, revocable, or permanent.

## 6. The report

The operative filed a report stating that contact had been established and that the asset had returned on independent terms. Every statement in the report is recorded as accurate.

The report does not describe the means by which contact was established, the substance of any discussion between the operative and the asset, or the operative's own assessment of the outcome. The record does not establish whether these omissions were deliberate.

General Ramcher read the report. The record does not describe his response. It establishes only that Anajak Forces thereafter contained an asset it could not task.

## Unresolved

- **The date:** the operation followed the establishment of Anajak Forces. No specific date is recorded.
- **The authorisation:** who authorised an operation the commanding general had refused to sign, and under what authority, is not established.
- **The assessment:** the basis on which the GIC concluded the asset was misplaced rather than committed is not recorded in detail.
- **The contact:** the substance of the operative's interaction with the asset is not recorded.
- **The report:** whether the operative's account is complete, and whether anyone in the GIC has assessed it as otherwise, is not established.
- **The terms:** whether the independent arrangement is revocable, and by whom, is not recorded.
- **The household:** whether LENDERMAIRE is aware that the asset has left, and whether it responded, is not established.
