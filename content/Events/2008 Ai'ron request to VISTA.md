# 2008 Ai'ron request to VISTA



<p class="lead">On 2 September 2008, approximately one week after the [[2008 Pocheasang Palace raid]], the government of the [[Ironland|Kingdom of the Ai'ron Land]] submitted a request to [[VISTA]] for the preparation and funding of military programmes in support of a full-scale campaign in the [[Ironclad DMZ]]. The request was referred to a vote of the twelve member delegations under the charter's two-thirds threshold, and failed.[^1]</p>

## 1. Background

Ai'ron was a member of VISTA and had drawn on VISTA funding for procurement and training. The raid of 25–26 August 2008 prompted a national lockdown and martial law, and the government cited the archival loss as the basis for emergency powers.

## 2. The request

The request asked VISTA to prepare and fund military programmes for a campaign into the Ironclad DMZ. Under VISTA's charter, funding and military programmes required a two-thirds majority — eight of the twelve delegations.

## 3. The vote

Six delegations voted in favour, four against, and two abstained. With eight votes required for authorisation, the proposal failed.

## 4. Positions

The recorded positions were not uniform within the opposition. The presidency's office, held by [[Noradus|Northrad]], stated that VISTA's instruments did not provide for funding offensive operations by a member into a stabilisation area, and that authorisation would require amendment of the charter. The remaining three opposing delegations issued separate statements.

## 5. Aftermath

Ai'ron remained a member of VISTA after the vote. The regional concern generated by the raid and by the disclosure of [[Konish's Group]] led VISTA to establish the [[Global Intervention Coalition (GIC)]] on 4 December 2008, with a mandate narrower than the funding request that had failed.
