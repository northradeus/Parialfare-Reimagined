# 2008 Ai'ron request to VISTA



On 2 September 2008, approximately one week after the [2008 Pocheasang Palace raid](/km/events/2008-pocheasang-palace-raid), the government of the [Kingdom of the Ai'ron Land](/locations/countries/ironland) submitted a request to [VISTA](/km/factions/vista) for the preparation and funding of military programmes in support of a full-scale campaign in the [Ironclad DMZ](/km/locations/ironclad-dmz). The request was referred to a vote of the twelve member delegations under the charter's two-thirds threshold, and failed.[^1]

## 1. Background

Ai'ron was a member of VISTA and had drawn on VISTA funding for procurement and training. The raid of 25–26 August 2008 prompted a national lockdown and martial law, and the government cited the archival loss as the basis for emergency powers.

## 2. The request

The request asked VISTA to prepare and fund military programmes for a campaign into the Ironclad DMZ. Under VISTA's charter, funding and military programmes required a two-thirds majority — eight of the twelve delegations.

## 3. The vote

Six delegations voted in favour, four against, and two abstained. With eight votes required for authorisation, the proposal failed.

## 4. Positions

The recorded positions were not uniform within the opposition. The presidency's office, held by [Northrad](/characters/vista/noradus), stated that VISTA's instruments did not provide for funding offensive operations by a member into a stabilisation area, and that authorisation would require amendment of the charter. The remaining three opposing delegations issued separate statements.

## 5. Aftermath

Ai'ron remained a member of VISTA after the vote. The regional concern generated by the raid and by the disclosure of [Konish's Group](/km/factions/konish's-group) led VISTA to establish the [Global Intervention Coalition (GIC)](/km/factions/global-intervention-coalition-(gic)) on 4 December 2008, with a mandate narrower than the funding request that had failed.
