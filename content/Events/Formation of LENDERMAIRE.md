# Formation of LENDERMAIRE



The **formation of [[LENDERMAIRE]]** refers to how [[Konish Kimbotin (Reimagined)|Konish Kimbotin]] assembled the group, within the umbrella coalition [[Konish's Group]], by selecting its members one at a time. The group is dated to 2007 and first became public in 2008.

## Selection
Konish chose each member for a specific reason. His working premise is that the more unhinged a person is, the better they are at certain things. Most of the members were considered mentally unstable before they joined him.

## Recruitment
Konish recruited members personally. [[Hanktonio]] describes him as a mysterious man who could not only "fix" people but also find them and persuade them to join. That is Hanktonio's account and is not independently confirmed.

Known or partly known recruitment histories:
- **[[Samsara Lykor|Lykor]]:** a former Lurkish SWAT officer and marine. A worker at a strip club where he was employed passed his résumé to Konish, who persuaded him to join despite Lykor's hostility toward Cladish people.
- **[[Markless]], [[Kaliber]] and [[Andrew]]:** a former squad of the Ironish Special Forces, with the group since early in its development. How they were recruited is not established.
- **[[Medic]]:** served another Cladish faction before joining. Which faction is not established.
- **[[Salmon]] and [[Vanny]]:** how Konish found Salmon and how he met Vanny are not established.

## Codenames
Members adopted codenames that distance them from their pasts.

## Public emergence
The group did not appear in open sources before the [[2008 Pocheasang Palace raid]]. Its emblem was recognised by Cladish individuals in Ai'ron from footage of the raid, and the name LENDERMAIRE entered reporting on 10 September 2008.

## Cohesion
Members have goals of their own. Konish merged them into a single goal that benefits the whole group. The goal itself is not defined. This is the principal basis of the members' loyalty to him.

The wider world has rejected the members because of their pasts and the mental health diagnoses they were given. Some never reached therapy. They have no other place to go. The group cannot exist without Konish, and Konish cannot exist without the group.

## The thirteenth member
The thirteenth member, [[Samsara Lykor|Lykor]], is the exception. He is not retained by the cohesion described above. He is rejected by the world but also sought by many, and he remains because of Konish by personal choice. His identity is concealed from the public.

## Unresolved

- Konish's own account of why he chose each member.
- The recruitment date of the thirteenth member.
- Whether the Ironish Special Forces squad joined together or separately.
- Where the group sits within Konish's Group's structure.
