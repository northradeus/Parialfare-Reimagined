# Parialfare: Reimagined

**Read this to understand the state of the world.** This is the master context file for the main continuity. It explains the geopolitical landscape, the major factions, the central conflict, and the hidden layers beneath them.

## Premise

Parialfare: Reimagined is a modern-warfare setting. It is grounded, messy, and heavily political. The year is 2008. The world is dominated by a technology race and an information war, fought through covert operations, contractors, and black-site facilities rather than open conventional war. The most powerful figures in the region are not states, but warlords, intelligence agencies, and private military companies.

## The Geopolitical Landscape

The world of 2008 is split between a fragile established order and an ungoverned frontier.

**[Ironland](/locations/countries/ironland)** (the Kingdom of the Ai'ron Land) is the established order. It is a constitutional monarchy with a population of 37 million, bordering the sea to the north and the frontier to the south. Its capital is [Pocheasang](/km/locations/pocheasang). It is a member of [VISTA](/km/factions/vista). The Crown retains supreme military authority, but day-to-day governance is split between the King and an elected Prime Minister.

**[Ironclad](/km/locations/countries/ironclad)** is the frontier. It is not a state. It is a vast, ungoverned demilitarised zone (the [Ironclad DMZ](/km/locations/ironclad-dmz)) ruled by competing warlords. There is no central government, no unified security force, and no rule of law beyond what local armed formations enforce. It is a haven for insurgencies, black markets, and displaced military assets.

**[New Aester](/km/locations/countries/new-aester)** sits on the border between them. It is a corporate city-state and an estate project built by Northrad, positioned directly between Ironland and the DMZ. It is a VISTA member and a crucial strategic buffer, populated by a mixed international citizenry under the watchful eye of the ITF.

**[Lurkinsberg](/km/locations/countries/lurkinsberg)** (demonym Lurkish) is a wealthy, disciplined post-colonial naval power. It dominates the sea lanes, maintains a large diaspora across the region, and serves as the primary source of the Anajak pipeline's assassins.

## The Major Factions

### The Institutional Order

**[VISTA](/km/factions/vista)** is the intergovernmental military organisation binding the region together. It is composed of twelve member delegations and requires a two-thirds majority to authorise military funding. Its presidency is held by [Northrad](/characters/vista/noradus), a 21-year-old former special operator and institutional architect.

**[Global Intervention Coalition (GIC)](/km/factions/global-intervention-coalition-(gic))** is VISTA's military arm, established in December 2008 and designed by Northrad. It is a coalition of generals and specialized forces. Do not confuse it with the ITF.

**[Anajak Forces](/km/factions/anajak-forces)** is the task force created under the GIC to combat the insurgency. It is led by [General Ramcher](/km/characters/anajak-forces/general-ramcher) and composed of highly trained assassins and combat specialists. Its classified core is labelled [Alpha-01](/km/factions/alpha-01) by the ITF. Many of its members are raised through the [Site-29](/km/locations/site-29) pipeline.

**[Ironish Task Force (ITF)](/factions/ironish-task-force-(itf))** is a private military contractor founded in 1998, headquartered in Pocheasang. It was originally contracted for static defense and close-quarter security, but during a recent coup, Northrad funded it heavily enough that it now answers largely to him. It effectively serves as his private army, guarding the brutalist edges of New Aester.

The two sides mirror each other. The [Global Intervention Coalition (GIC)](/km/factions/global-intervention-coalition-(gic)) mirrors [Konish's Group](/km/factions/konish's-group) — one coalition meeting another. Beneath them, [Anajak Forces](/km/factions/anajak-forces) mirrors [LENDERMAIRE](/km/factions/lendermaire) — the task force meeting the black operations unit. Every institution has its counterpart across the line.

### The Insurgency

**[Konish's Group](/km/factions/konish's-group)** is the primary threat to the institutional order. It is a transnational Cladish umbrella coalition of autonomous militias operating out of the Ironclad DMZ. It is led by the warlord [Konish Kimbotin](/characters/lendermaire/konish-kimbotin-(reimagined)), whose primary weapon is information.

**[LENDERMAIRE](/km/factions/lendermaire)** is Konish's black operations unit. It is a thirteen-member clandestine formation garrisoned as a single shared household in southern Ironclad. It is structurally anomalous: no standard rank structure, no external oversight, and members selected for psychological deviation rather than standardized aptitude. Its second-in-command is [Hanktonio](/km/characters/lendermaire/hanktonio).

## The Central Conflict: 2008

The defining event of the era is the **[2008 Pocheasang Palace raid](/km/events/2008-pocheasang-palace-raid)** (25–26 August).

LENDERMAIRE launched a frontal assault on the Pocheasang palace complex — the seat of the Ai'ron government and the site of the consolidated national archives. They breached the perimeter, engaged the defending [Laopanster Gendarmerie](/km/factions/laopanster-gendarmerie) and the contracted ITF, and extracted classified military deployment plans and hard drives. Twelve defenders were killed.

The raid exposed a massive security failure. In its aftermath, Ironland declared martial law. On 2 September, Ironland petitioned [VISTA](/km/factions/vista) to fund a full-scale military campaign into the Ironclad DMZ. The request failed by a vote of six to four, with two abstentions, partly because VISTA's charter does not provide for funding offensive operations. This failure forced VISTA to establish the GIC and Anajak Forces as a workaround.

The raid also revealed the existence of LENDERMAIRE, which had previously been unknown in open sources. VISTA's Intelligence Agency subsequently disclosed Konish's Group, and the region began to militarize for a shadow war.

## The Hidden Layers

Beneath the geopolitical struggle are the black sites and the variants.

**[Site-29](/km/locations/site-29)** is a heavily concealed training installation operating outside official oversight. It psychologically conditions and trains covert operatives. Its graduates include [Tanishito](/km/characters/anajak-forces/tanishito), [Nogo](/characters/anajak-forces/nogo), and [Pepper](/km/characters/anajak-forces/pepper). It deliberately isolates its subjects so they share no collective history. The institution that runs it remains unknown.

**The Variants.** This continuity is one of several. Many characters here are variants of characters in other continuities (e.g., Konish (Classic), Leepy (Classic)). A variant is a separate entity. Do not import facts or history from another continuity unless explicitly linked. See Variants at the vault root.

## Quick Reference

**Key Figures:**
* **[Northrad](/characters/vista/noradus):** President of VISTA. Architect of the GIC and Anajak.
* **[Konish Kimbotin](/characters/lendermaire/konish-kimbotin-(reimagined)):** Cladish warlord. Leader of Konish's Group and LENDERMAIRE.
* **[Hanktonio](/km/characters/lendermaire/hanktonio):** Konish's second-in-command. "The Second Hand".
* **[Samsara Lykor](/km/characters/lendermaire/samsara-lykor):** LENDERMAIRE's concealed thirteenth member. The other "Northrad".
* **[General Ramcher](/km/characters/anajak-forces/general-ramcher):** Commander of Anajak Forces.
* **[Tanishito](/km/characters/anajak-forces/tanishito) & [Nogo](/characters/anajak-forces/nogo):** S-grade and A+ grade assassins of Anajak.
* **[Pepper](/km/characters/anajak-forces/pepper):** Anajak operator and Northrad's personal adjutant.

**Key Locations:**
* **[Pocheasang](/km/locations/pocheasang):** Capital of Ironland. Site of the raid.
* **[Ironclad DMZ](/km/locations/ironclad-dmz):** The ungoverned frontier.
* **[Site-29](/km/locations/site-29):** The black-site training facility.

**Key Concepts:**
* **[Anajak grading system](/km/lore/anajak-grading-system):** S, A+, A, etc. Used to classify facility-raised operatives.
* **The Chamber:** Ironland's bicameral legislature.
