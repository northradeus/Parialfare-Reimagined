# Kingdom of Encore

The **Kingdom of Encore** is a sovereign constitutional monarchy located on the eastern expanse of the Parial continent and a member state of [VISTA](/km/factions/vista). Historically colonized by [Lurkinsberg](/km/locations/countries/lurkinsberg) and heavily influenced by [Ai'ron](/locations/countries/ironland) trade, modern Encore balances institutional integration into VISTA coalitions with persistent internal militarization alongside the [Black Kroma](/km/factions/black-kroma).

## 1. History & Colonial Influence

Encore was historically placed under colonial administration by [Lurkinsberg](/km/locations/countries/lurkinsberg), which established centralized civil service systems, riverine transport corridors, and legal codes. Following the contraction of direct Lurkish colonial rule, the Kingdom of Ai'ron (Ironland) established dominant commercial and diplomatic ties throughout the territory.

As a consequence of this dual historical exposure, both Lurkish and Ironish are spoken with high administrative fluency among Encorean civil servants, diplomats, and military officers. Military personnel entering multinational commands routinely demonstrate multi-language operational competence, as observed in operators such as [Pich](/km/characters/anajak-forces/pich).

## 2. Internal Security & The Black Kroma

During the 20th century, Encore endured internal conflict involving the [Black Kroma](/km/factions/black-kroma), a radical totalitarian insurgent regime responsible for mass internment, interrogation facilities, and border purges (the period from which historic figures like Chan Rithipol originated).

In the contemporary era, the Black Kroma has evolved from an ideological insurgent regime into an entrenched, recognized military faction holding standing armaments. Rather than an ongoing civil war, a tense, legally codified coexistence exists between the Encorean Royal Army and the Black Kroma apparatus. Persistent security concerns surrounding this armed balance formed the primary motivation for Encore's accession into [VISTA](/km/factions/vista).

## 3. VISTA Integration & The Anajak Agreement

Following the founding of the [Global Intervention Coalition (GIC)](/km/factions/global-intervention-coalition-(gic)), member delegations sought standardized forward deployment protocols. VISTA President [General Northrad](/characters/vista/noradus) conducted an official state visit to Encore to ratify mutual defense and border observation terms.

Concurrent with public treaty negotiations, Northrad concluded a confidential bilateral security understanding regarding regional intervention rights. This diplomatic exchange is historically linked to the designation of [Anajak Forces](/km/factions/anajak-forces), adopting the Encorean term *Anajak* (denoting sovereign authority or realm) for the covert intervention task force commanded by [General Ramcher](/km/characters/anajak-forces/general-ramcher).

## 4. Military Doctrine

The Kingdom of Encore maintains the **Encorean Royal Army**, which operates the standardized **Royal Academy Close-Quarters System**. This formalized martial curriculum integrates traditional Encorean close-combat techniques with modern battlefield utility, centered on tactical knife combat and synchronized fire-team maneuvers. This institutionalized doctrine is maintained separately from historic wartime irregular methods such as the bladed tonfa disciplines of earlier generations.
