# Ironclad



**Ironclad** is an ungoverned territory between the [Kingdom of the Ai'ron Land](/locations/countries/ironland) and its neighbours. It is not a state. It has no central government, no unified security force, and no authority exercising jurisdiction across it. Competing warlords rule it. Its people are the Cladish.

The territory was conquered by an empire, abandoned six years later, and left to itself for a hundred and ten years. Its people named it Ironclad themselves, after the abandonment.

## 1. History

### 1.1 The Deck

Before its conquest the territory was the seat of the Deck Empire. The Deck absorbed or extinguished thirty-two percent of the world's ethnic groups. Its way of life was heavily militarized, and its leaders were warlords.

The Deck is not confined to this territory in the record. Its people were called the Dekish in the medieval Rogolfenstine era, and its martial tradition is recorded as the inheritance of the Cladish.

### 1.2 The conquest

The Ai'ross Empire — the imperial phase of the Kingdom of the Ai'ron Land — conquered the Deck during the Balthazar Era, the medieval war that broke it. The territory was renamed Ai'ronclad. The Deck Empire ceased to exist as a state.

### 1.3 The abandonment

Ai'ross itself fell in the late medieval era, when Ironland was founded from its remains. The frontier was then held by Ironland, which governed it for centuries.

In 1898, Ironland withdrew from the Cladish frontier and left the territory to itself.

The Cladish adopted the word *Ironclad* after the abandonment, as a badge of survival. The name is a self-name. It was not imposed by the Ironish.

### 1.4 Isolation

The territory has been isolated for a hundred and ten years. No external power has governed it since 1898. What occurred within it during that period is not recorded.

### 1.5 Survival of absorbed populations

Outside institutions historically recorded thirty-two percent of the region's ethnic groups as extinguished or fully assimilated by the Deck Empire and the subsequent Balthazar Era, notably including the Jennies.

In the interior of Ironclad, many absorbed traditions, cultural practices, and lineages survived. Following the 1898 abandonment, the persistent danger and lack of governance in the territory prevented external states and academic bodies from conducting systematic ethnographic surveys. Lineages that external archives recorded as extinct persisted in the southern districts, intermarrying with the general population and producing distinct mixed identities, including the Cladish-Jens.

## 2. The warlord inheritance

The Cladish are the descendants of the Deck. Their politics are the politics of the warlord, and their way of life is heavily militarized.

The record does not establish whether this is a surviving cultural trait of the Deck or an adaptation to a century of isolation. It states only that the pattern continues.

The wars of Ironclad are not wars between states. They are wars between armed formations over territory, resources, and the question of what Ironclad should become.

## 3. The unification question

Two visions compete for the future of the territory.

**[Konish Kimbotin](/characters/lendermaire/konish-kimbotin-(reimagined))** seeks to unify the militias into a single armed force, the CAF, and to build an actual society out of the wreckage. He is a warlord who wants Ironclad to become a state.

**Maire O'Mare** seeks to revive the Rogolfenstine era, the medieval deep past of Nolheim and the age of Koilen Rogolf, the Supreme Leader of the Maire O'Mare movement. In 2008 Maire O'Mare is an insurgency that has adapted that ideology to modern warfare. It is not the person returning. It is the idea.

Konish regards Maire O'Mare as extreme, and as a threat capable of outlasting his own coalition. He has prioritised its destruction over its absorption. He fears the figure and the name because of Rogolfenstine.

## 4. Geography

The [Ironclad DMZ](/locations/ironclad-dmz) lies within the territory, on its northern approach to [Ironland](/locations/countries/ironland). The [New Aester](/locations/countries/new-aester) city-state sits on the northern border, between Ironland and the zone.

In the southern districts armed formations exercise quasi-governmental functions — taxation, adjudication, and conscription — on a local basis. [Konish's Group](/factions/konish's-group) holds territory there.

## Unresolved

- **The Deck's own name:** what the Deck called the land, and what the Ironish called it during the six years of rule, are not recorded. *Ironclad* is the Cladish self-name of 1898. The names of the *empire* are recorded in Rogolfenstine.
- **The extent of the Deck:** its full reach, and which of the world's ethnic groups it absorbed or extinguished, are not recorded. While outside records classified many absorbed groups as extinct, surviving lineages in the interior remain largely undocumented.
- **The conquest and collapse:** the circumstances of the 1892 conquest and the 1898 collapse are not established.
- **The isolation:** what occurred in Ironclad between 1898 and 2008 is not recorded.
- **The warlords:** how many there are, and which, is not established.
- **Konish's position:** whether he holds any official position in Ironclad is unresolved.
