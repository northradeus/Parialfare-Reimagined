# New Aester



<p class="lead">**New Aester** is a corporate city-state situated on the border between the [[Ironland|Kingdom of the Ai'ron Land]] and the [[Ironclad DMZ]]. It is a member of [[VISTA]] and operates as a heavily policed proving ground for corporate technologies. The state was built as an estate project by [[Noradus|Northrad]], who serves as its architect and founder. It is populated by a diverse, mixed-nationality citizenry drawn by the promise of a highly advanced smart city.</p>

The country functions as a crucial strategic buffer between the established order of Ironland and the ungoverned territories of the Cladish warlords. Its outer edges are heavily militarized, garrisoned by the [[Ironish Task Force (ITF)]].

## 1. The Corporate Experiment

New Aester is funded and operated as a corporate project under Northrad. The state serves as a proving ground for various technologies, which are tested on the resident population. The country's infrastructure, including its educational institutions, is recorded as highly futuristic.

The resident population is recorded as believing the city is a highly advanced smart city. The record does not state whether the citizens are aware of their status as test subjects for Northrad's ventures.

## 2. Government and Politics

New Aester is governed as a corporate technocracy. While Northrad is the architect and founder, administrative authority is exercised by the New Aester City Council. The city-state is a member of VISTA, having been designated alongside other nations following the 2008 Pocheasang Palace raid.

## 3. Geography and the Divide

The country is physically divided. The interior comprises the polished infrastructure of the corporate experiment, featuring advanced smart-city technologies. The outer edge is described as brutalist.

The [[Ironish Task Force (ITF)]] resides and deploys from New Aester. Its forces are pushed out to the brutalist edges of the city, where they guard the wall that separates the interior from the DMZ.

## 4. Demographics

Unlike the Kingdom of the Ai'ron Land, which has a majority Ironish population, New Aester is populated by a mixed international citizenry. The exact demographic percentages are not recorded, but the population includes Ironish, Lurkish, and Cladish individuals, alongside corporate expatriates from across the VISTA member states.

The ITF is recorded as viewing the resident population as naive, soft, and unaware of the reality outside the city walls. The ITF operates as the physical cordon between the experiment and the ungoverned territory. The record establishes no social overlap between the soldiers on the wall and the citizens inside.

## 5. The Frontline

New Aester sits directly between Ironland and the Ironclad DMZ. It does not touch the DMZ itself.

During the events of 2008, Northrad declined a full-scale invasion of the Ironclad DMZ because open war would put New Aester on the frontline. The country has plans to expand across the South Ironclad Region.

## Unresolved

- **The technology:** The specific technologies being tested on the population are not recorded.
- **Citizen awareness:** Whether the residents know they are test subjects, or genuinely believe the city is a standard smart city, is unresolved.
- **Governance:** The exact legislative structure and the city council's relationship to Northrad are not established.
- **Demographics:** The exact population numbers and the full list of nationalities present in the state are not recorded.
- **The wall:** Whether the ITF has ever been required to suppress the civilian population, or if the wall has ever been breached, is not recorded.
