# Ironclad DMZ



The **Ironclad DMZ** is an ungoverned demilitarised zone between [Ai'ron](locations/countries/ironland) and its neighbours. The territory has no central government, unified security force, or authority exercising jurisdiction across it.

In the southern districts, armed formations exercise quasi-governmental functions — taxation, adjudication, and conscription — on a local basis. [Konish's Group](factions/konish's-group) holds territory across the south. The border city of [New Aester](locations/countries/new-aester) lies between Ai'ron and the zone.

## Strategic Fortifications and Internal Geography

The geography of the zone is split between its fortified northern frontier and an undocumented interior:

*   **Area-31 (Shaiken):** Located along the northern perimeter directly facing [New Aester](locations/countries/new-aester), Area-31 is the most heavily fortified and advanced stronghold in the DMZ. Built on the scale of a small city with monolithic brutalist architecture, the base maintains high-density anti-aircraft missile arrays and hardened deployment hangars for supersonic fighter aircraft. Its defensive mass is the primary operational factor that delayed New Aester's planned Southern Frontier Expansion.
*   **The Protected Southern Interior:** Deep behind Konish's military perimeter lie settled civilian communities and municipal institutions. These populations operate under the protection of Konish's Group and remain undocumented by foreign observers, as external ground forces and aerial surveillance cannot penetrate the perimeter.

Following the [2008 Pocheasang Palace raid](events/2008-pocheasang-palace-raid), Ai'ron petitioned [VISTA](factions/vista) on 2 September 2008 to fund a full-scale military campaign into the DMZ. The request failed to reach the two-thirds voting threshold.
