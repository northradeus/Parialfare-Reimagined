# Anajak grading system



<p class="lead">Personnel of [[Anajak Forces]] are graded. The system is not fully documented, and its criteria, scale and authority are unestablished.</p>

## Observed grades

Grades seen in the record so far are **S+, S, A+ and A**.

* [[Tanishito]] — S-grade assassin.
* [[Nogo]] — A+ grade assassin.
* [[Pepper]] — S+ at multitasking, A tier in combat.
* [[Verdanski]] — A+.

Because Pepper carries different grades for different skills, per-skill grading appears to exist alongside overall ranks. This is a reading of the data, not a stated rule.

## Unresolved

- **Authority:** who assigns grades.
- **Scale:** the full scale, including top and bottom grades.
- **Granularity:** whether grades are overall, per skill, or both.
- **Criteria:** what the criteria are.
