# Hayakawa Oradachi

**Hayakawa Oradachi** is a [Thaiphonese](/locations/countries/thaiphon) fighter pilot of the Cladish Armed Forces air wing, based at [Area-31](/locations/area-31) in Shaiken. He flies one of the supersonic aircraft the coalition calls "The Sun". He is known to the coalition only by his callsign, **Infinity**, which the Cladish air wing renders as **Aifensto**. His real name appears in no [VISTA](/factions/vista) or [GIC](/factions/global-intervention-coalition-(gic)) record. He is killed in action during Operation Numbrad.

## 1. Racing career and the ban

Oradachi was a professional race car driver who competed in the same circuit as [Takahashi](/characters/anajak-forces/takahashi) ("Sahara"). He was **banned from racing**. He was not retired. The record does not state the reason for the ban, the body that issued it, or whether he was guilty of what he was banned for.

His father was in hospital during his career. The family's money went into that career. His father never saw him win a championship, and died shortly after the money ran out. The record states these facts and nothing about how Oradachi carried them. He is not recorded as having spoken of them to anyone.

Racing conditioned his body for sustained G-load, as it did Takahashi's. The two are not recorded as having known each other.

## 2. Recruitment and training

Oradachi is Thaiphonese. [Konish's Group](/factions/konish's-group) recruited him abroad, which means the Group's reach extends into [Thaiphon](/locations/countries/thaiphon) itself. Only [Konish](/characters/lendermaire/konish-kimbotin-(reimagined)) is recorded as knowing this. Thaiphon, a VISTA member that opposed the Ironclad invasion, has no record of one of its citizens flying for a designated destabilising group.

He learned to fly at [Area-31](/locations/area-31), inside the Cladish Armed Forces' own supersonic programme. Ironclad has no flight schools. The record does not describe the programme, his instructors, or his training time. He is not among the thirteen of [LENDERMAIRE](/factions/lendermaire) and has no recorded place in the household.

He fits the pattern of Konish's recruiting: a man an institution wrote off, found by Konish. The record does not state how he was found.

## 3. The callsign

"Infinity" is the coalition's rendering. "Aifensto" is the Cladish term. Who chose the name is not recorded. It may be defiance, or it may be a verdict, and the record does not say which. Sahara's callsign is [Lurkish](/locations/countries/lurkinsberg), so [Anajak](/factions/anajak-forces) names its pilots in its command language, and Konish's air wing names its own in Cladish.

## 4. Operation Numbrad

Infinity's aircraft dumps Flash Pallets repeatedly while he pulls hard manoeuvres against Takahashi's. The dazzle blinds the ground troops of the [ITF](/factions/ironish-task-force-(itf)) and coalition contingents, who need it stopped.

During the dogfight, the force of the manoeuvres puts him into G-LOC. A hit to the aircraft with little penetration lands while he is unconscious, enough to make the Flash Pallets combust inside the airframe. His death is instant. He never knows.

The aircraft pulls itself up, flies on without him, and runs out of fuel somewhere in [Ironclad](/locations/countries/ironclad). Konish's Group recovers the wreck, so VISTA never obtains a wreck or a name. The coalition logs the loss as an unidentified Cladish airframe.

No one is credited with the kill. Takahashi's only confirmed kill remains [Salmon](/characters/lendermaire/salmon).

## 5. Legacy

The coalition never learns his name. The fear he caused is attributed to the aircraft and to "The Sun". Whether anyone at Area-31 mourns him is not recorded, and his name is not among those [Joey](/characters/lendermaire/joey) reads at the mass funeral, because he belonged to Area-31, not the household.

## Unresolved

- **The ban:** its reason, its issuer, and whether he was guilty.
- **Takahashi:** whether the two ever raced the same circuit. The record establishes only that they do not know each other in the dogfight.
- **The hit:** what struck the aircraft. The ground troops' wish to end the flares is the only motive on record. Proposed, not canon: fragments from the same battery that downed Takahashi.
- **Auto-recovery and pallet storage:** how the aircraft pulled itself up, and why a combustion inside the airframe did not bring it down. A matter for the unresolved Area-31 engineering.
- **The callsign:** who chose it.
- **Mourning:** whether anyone at Area-31 remembers him.
