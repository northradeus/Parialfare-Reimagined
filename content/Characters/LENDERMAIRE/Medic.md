# Medic



**Medic** is the combat medic of the black operations unit [[LENDERMAIRE]]. He served another Cladish faction before joining; which faction is not established.

No personal name is recorded for him. *Medic* is a designation, not a name, and the record does not establish whether he has one, whether he uses it, or whether any member of the household has ever asked.

He is the only member of the household recorded as having been inside another faction, and the only one whose past did not end with his leaving it. It ended when [[Konish's Group]] absorbed it.

## 1. The faction

The faction Medic served is not named in the record. What is established is that it was Cladish, that it no longer exists as an independent body, and that it was absorbed by Konish's Group.

The record does not describe the absorption: whether it was negotiated, coerced, or simply the last option available to a faction that had run out of others. It does not record whether the faction resisted.

## 2. The absorption

Absorption is the mechanism by which the coalition grows. Konish's Group is recorded as a network of autonomous militias, and that network expands by taking in formations that can no longer stand alone.

When a faction is absorbed, its people become assets of the absorbing body. They are folded in, reassigned, or discarded. The record does not describe how those decisions are made, or who makes them.

Medic was kept. The record does not establish whether he was kept deliberately, as a valuable trade, or by accident, as one of the people who happened to still be standing when the paperwork was done.

This is the seam between the coalition's appetite and Kimbotin's personal practice. The Group absorbs the faction; Kimbotin collects the people left over. Medic is what it looks like when both happen to the same man.

## 3. The silence

Medic does not speak about the faction he came from, and the record does not establish why.

It is not recorded as a secret. Nothing in the record suggests he is hiding what happened, or that the household has ever asked. He is recorded as quiet about his past in the same way the rest of the household is quiet about theirs — which is to say, entirely, and without anyone finding it remarkable.

The record does not describe his work during the absorption, and does not establish whether he treated casualties on either side of it.

## 4. The trade

Medic is the unit's combat medic. His function is recorded and his history is not.

He is the only member of the household whose professional skill is directly transferable to a stranger. Anyone can be taught to shoot. Not anyone can be taught to keep a man alive in the dark with what is in the bag.

The record does not establish whether he has ever trained another member of the household, or whether he would.

## 5. The chair

Medic sits where nobody can come up behind him.

The household reads this, if it reads it at all, as habit. The record does not describe where the habit came from, and does not establish whether it predates the absorption.

## Unresolved

- **The name:** no personal name is recorded. Whether one existed, whether he uses it, and whether the household has ever asked are not established.
- **Prior faction:** which Cladish faction he served before joining LENDERMAIRE, and what became of the people in it who were not kept.
- **The absorption:** whether it was negotiated or coerced, and whether he was kept deliberately or by accident.
- **The grievance:** whether he holds any grievance about being absorbed rather than released, and whether he has ever expressed one.
- **The silence:** whether it is discretion, resignation, or something he has not been asked about.
- **The training:** whether he has ever taught another member of the household, or been asked to.
- **The chair:** whether the habit predates the absorption.
