# Vanny



**Vanny** is the youngest of the thirteen core members of the black operations unit [LENDERMAIRE](/factions/lendermaire), serving as a technical specialist in cyber operations, electronic warfare and logistics. Unlike conventional technical personnel who operate from secured facilities, Vanny is regularly deployed alongside LENDERMAIRE's assault elements, carrying portable jamming equipment and his signature ruggedised laptop into the field. He has an androgynous, age-ambiguous appearance and combines military practicality with streetwear in his field loadout.

## 1. Specialisation

Vanny's responsibilities bridge three areas.

**Cyber operations.** Digital information acquisition and management; technical exploitation of recovered information; computer-based operations; digital concealment and information handling.

**Electronic warfare.** Portable communications jamming; signal monitoring; deployment and maintenance of electronic warfare equipment; experimentation with electronic equipment, including experimental electromagnetic devices.

**Logistics.** Technical equipment management; procurement and resource acquisition; maintenance and equipment allocation; maintaining operational resource records.

His existing roster annotation, "Hackinton & Resource," remains unexplained.

## 2. Field deployment and equipment

Vanny is not restricted to a command post. He regularly accompanies LENDERMAIRE's combat elements into operational environments, preferring electronic warfare first and conventional combat second: he attempts to interfere with the technological systems available to opposing forces while retaining the ability to participate in direct engagements. His primary firearm is a compact assault rifle.

His clothing combines military practicality with streetwear — an oversized hoodie or modified tactical jacket over a tactical vest, headphones or personal audio equipment, practical trousers and combat boots, personalised equipment attachments, and the ruggedised laptop as part of his field loadout. Exact physical features, hair and eye colour remain undecided. See *Appearance (proposed)* below.

His ruggedised laptop is his most recognisable personal possession — a portable technical workstation, and not simply another piece of issued equipment. His attachment to it is considerable; it represents his technical identity. Vanny is an experimentalist rather than a collector: his interest is in finding out what something can do.

## Appearance (proposed)

*Proposed material. The record fixes Vanny's clothing and equipment and describes his appearance as androgynous and age-ambiguous. It does not describe his face or build.*

Vanny's clothing is cut larger than he is. The hoodie or modified jacket hangs past the wrist and sits loose over the tactical vest, so the layers hide his frame and give no reliable read of his build. Visitors meeting him for the first time guess his sex and age differently, and the household has stopped correcting them.

Vanny is Thaiphonese. The record does not describe how this shows in his appearance.

*Hair, eye colour and exact features remain undecided.*

## 3. Operation: Sparta (25–26 August 2008)

Vanny was already a member of LENDERMAIRE during the [2008 Pocheasang Palace raid](/events/2008-pocheasang-palace-raid). His specialisation makes him a relevant participant given the operation's confirmed use of portable jamming equipment, though the surviving record does not detail his specific actions. His role likely encompassed one or more of the following:

1. **Electronic warfare operator** — deployment and management of portable jamming equipment.
2. **Digital material handler** — technical handling and identification of recovered digital storage devices.
3. **Technical combatant** — accompanying the assault element with specialised equipment and a conventional firearm.

## 4. Conduct and temperament

Vanny's communication is informal, sarcastic, playful and heavily shaped by online culture; he uses internet slang, gaming references and memes in ordinary conversation. He is impulsive, easily distracted when something more interesting catches his attention, mischievous, and technically curious to the point of compulsion — he wants to test unfamiliar equipment and sometimes underestimates the risks of doing so. He struggles with traditional behavioural expectations and authority.

His psychological difficulties are not merely an extension of his humour. He has genuine social and behavioural difficulties alongside attention and impulse-control issues. His performance is inconsistent. When interested in a technical challenge he can produce exceptional results; mundane responsibilities, repetitive assignments and situations requiring sustained discipline are harder for him. He occasionally requires direction from [Konish](/characters/lendermaire/konish-kimbotin-(reimagined)) or [Hanktonio](/characters/lendermaire/hanktonio). This does not make him a background technician or an inferior combatant — he is expected to perform as a full member of LENDERMAIRE.

## 5. Background

Vanny was recruited into LENDERMAIRE before August 2008. Konish recognised his raw technical ability despite his behavioural difficulties and unconventional background, and considered his capabilities more important than his lack of conventional discipline, giving him an environment in which his abilities could be used rather than treated as a liability. His technical abilities are largely self-developed, built from experimentation, online communities, software manipulation and electronics.

The precise circumstances of their first meeting, and the nature of Vanny's previous activities, remain undocumented.[^1]

### Background (proposed)

*Proposed material. The record holds no account of Vanny's activities before LENDERMAIRE. This section fills that acknowledged blank and is not recovered record.*

His reputation as an internet nuisance was a reputation for inconvenience rather than danger. He took apart what was not his, found the seams in systems that were meant to be closed, and told people about it. The institutions that examined him recorded the difficulties, because the difficulties were measurable, and did not record the ability, because it was not.

Konish read the same facts in the opposite order. How he found Vanny is not recorded, and the record should not supply it.

No one is recorded as having come looking for Vanny afterward.

## Depiction

His morning in the kitchen, his argument with Joey over the cupboard, and his ascent to the roof to deliver a plate are dramatised in The Youngest. The scene is narrative, not record. It is also the fullest surviving account of the household at rest. It records the clearest instance of Vanny admiring a piece of equipment for what it lacks rather than what it can do: the spotting scope on the roof, which has no electronics in it, and which he calls beautiful.
