# Bizmo



**Bizmo** is a background operator of the black operations unit [LENDERMAIRE](/factions/lendermaire), responsible for 3D printing and the engineering of unconventional gadgets, accessories, and equipment designed for warfare. He is not counted among the group's thirteen core members, and works from the workshop rather than deploying in the field. He is an outsider to the group's core membership, and was recruited through a connection to [Vanny](/characters/lendermaire/vanny), with whom he collaborates on hardware projects. Vanny's field is cyber operations, electronic warfare, and logistics; the division of responsibility between the two is maintained on a project basis.

## Specialisation

Bizmo specialises in 3D printing and unconventional field fabrication. His work concerns the production of hardware through workshop-based processes.

His assignment is non-deployable. He does not accompany LENDERMAIRE personnel on combat deployments.

The precise range of equipment produced by Bizmo is not recorded.

*Proposed:* Bizmo maintains a dedicated inventory of printable components and fabrication materials. Completed projects are categorised by compatibility and intended use.

*Proposed:* Fabrication work is conducted through a combination of digital modelling, physical assembly, and post-production modification.

## Association with Vanny

Bizmo's recruitment is connected to Vanny. The circumstances of this connection are not recorded.

The two collaborate on hardware projects. The exact division of responsibility is not established. No formal supervisory relationship between the two is established.

*Proposed:* Vanny handles technical requirements and hardware integration. Bizmo handles physical production, component modification, and workshop assembly.

## Household Assignment

Bizmo resides with LENDERMAIRE personnel on land owned by [Konish Kimbotin (Reimagined)](/characters/lendermaire/konish-kimbotin-(reimagined)) in the southern [Ironclad DMZ](/locations/ironclad-dmz). He is a background operator and is not included among the thirteen core members. Background personnel are treated as members of the household's family unit despite their exclusion from the core membership count.

His daily schedule and domestic responsibilities are not recorded.

*Proposed:* Bizmo's workshop operates as a permanent household facility. Access to its equipment is coordinated with Vanny when projects require shared hardware.

## Operational Classification

Bizmo is not field-deployable. His contribution is restricted to workshop production and fabrication.

No combat deployment, direct weapons assignment, or field attachment is established.

His relationship to LENDERMAIRE's operational command structure is not documented beyond his background-operator status.
