# Samsara Lykor



<p class="lead">**Samsara Lykor** (born 1987) is a [[Lurkish]] operator and the concealed thirteenth member of [[LENDERMAIRE]]. A former [[Lurkish]] SWAT officer and marine, he is an independent operator who completes unfinished objectives and serves as the team's backup sniper. He is known publicly only by the designation "Northrad."</p>

Lykor is a different individual from [[Noradus]], the president of [[VISTA]]. The two share a designation through the [[Military naming custom]] and have no established personal or organizational connection.

## Designation

"Northrad" is an uncommon military designation, associated with soldiers regarded as having the military as their destiny.

Lykor received it from his squad leader, who recognized his exceptional marksmanship and the fellowship he showed toward his fellow soldiers. The name reflected both his individual skill and his reputation within the squad.

## Appearance

Lykor is pale and dark-haired, with a lean build that his civilian clothes conceal. He is recorded as looking younger than his service record suggests, a fact that often causes opponents to underestimate him.

The portrait of him from the 2008 period shows him with a bandaged brow and heavy bruising across the nose and cheek. He does not look like a man who has just won a fight. He looks like a man who has just finished one, regardless of the cost to his body.

His expression is recorded as flat and unreadable. This is not the lethargic warmth he exhibits in the household; it is the complete absence of reaction to violence. He is recorded as taking severe damage in combat without apparent concern, a trait his file attributes to his psychological diagnosis. He does not move like a man avoiding a hit; he moves like a man who knows he can survive one.

## Military service

Lykor first served as a Lurkish SWAT officer. At 22 he volunteered for the Lurkish Army and became a full-time Lurkish marine.

In service he earned a reputation for surviving some of the deadliest missions, including raids on enemy compounds and skirmishes against Cladish terrorists. His marksmanship and survival brought him considerable recognition.

He received more praise in the army than he had in his police work, and that recognition encouraged him to stay on as a full-time marine. He went on to undergo extreme training and take part in increasingly dangerous missions.

## Psychological evaluation and demotion

After a particularly brutal raid, Lykor was requested to attend a therapy session. The diagnosis identified:
- Individualism
- Narcissism
- Psychopathy

The findings led to his demotion. His behavior and individualistic tendencies directly endangered his team, and his effectiveness as an individual soldier could not offset the risk he posed to group operations. This was a turning point in his military career.

## Life after military service

Lykor became a bodyguard at a strip club owned by gang members connected to [[Konish Kimbotin (Reimagined)|Konish]].

He was struggling financially and accumulating debt. He was also paranoid that law enforcement would issue arrest warrants after he stole military equipment issued to him during his service. He wanted a way out of his circumstances and a way to distance himself from his past.

A worker at the establishment passed his résumé to Konish. This is how Konish became aware of him.

## Recruitment into LENDERMAIRE

Konish found Lykor while he was struggling financially and looking for an escape from his previous life.

Lykor hates Cladish people. Konish persuaded him to join anyway, and Lykor became the thirteenth member. The arrangement is unusual: he joined an organization led by a man from a group he fundamentally hated. *See also [[Formation of LENDERMAIRE]].*

## Alternative allegiance

Lykor's Lurkish military background places him on [[Ironish Task Force (ITF)|ITF]]'s recruitment list. His continued involvement with LENDERMAIRE is an alternative to joining ITF. If Konish were removed from the equation, Lykor could potentially work for ITF.

This is a key difference between Lykor and the other twelve members: he has an alternative outside LENDERMAIRE and chooses to stay.

## The thirteenth seat

LENDERMAIRE maintains thirteen core members in addition to [[Konish Kimbotin (Reimagined)|Konish Kimbotin]]. It is publicly known that the unit has thirteen; it is not publicly known who the thirteenth is. The seat is associated publicly only with the designation "Northrad," and the identity of its holder remains a point of speculation and internal tension.

The thirteenth member is deployed alone because he is considered more dangerous when operating independently. The assignment is not recorded as a punishment or a sign of distrust; it is recorded as an acknowledgement of his operational strengths. His principal responsibility is to finish what LENDERMAIRE leaves unfinished. The precise operational meaning of "finishing the job" is not established.

His position holds three contradictions at once. He belongs to a group that requires a central leader, but operates independently of its cohesion. He has an alternative outside the organisation, but remains. He keeps his distance from the others, and stays.

The record notes that his existence makes the dependence of members 1–12 on Kimbotin more visible.

## Relationships

### [[Konish Kimbotin (Reimagined)|Konish Kimbotin]]
Konish brought Lykor into LENDERMAIRE. The relationship is significant because Lykor's hatred of Cladish people was outweighed by a personal connection to Konish.

### [[LENDERMAIRE]]
Lykor is the thirteenth member. Unlike members 1–12, he does not depend on the cohesion mechanism to remain. He operates independently and keeps his membership by choice.

### [[Noradus]]
No personal or organizational connection is established.

## Unresolved

- **2008 raid participation:** Born in 1987 and enlisting at 22, in 2009, he would still have been a police officer in August 2008. Either his dates or his participation in the raid need to change.
- **Roster numbering:** records conflict on whether Andrew and Lykor are the twelfth and thirteenth members.
- **"Finishing the job":** the precise operational meaning of the phrase is not established.

## Depiction

His stay in the household, and his own account of why he remains, are dramatised in [[The Thirteenth]]. The scene is narrative, not record; the facts it turns on — the locked door, the Task Force's offer, his refusal of it — are consistent with the article.

His recruitment of [[Tanishito]] from the strip club where he once worked, and his quiet dismantling of her performance on the drive to the helipad, are dramatised in [[The Lot]]. He mistakes her for a broken veteran, not a spy.

His departure from the household, and the terms on which he returned to the GIC, are dramatised in the reference draft of [[Operation Fallow — Episode]]. The draft is not canon and its plot is provisional, but it is the fullest reading of how he behaves when he decides something.

For a synthesis of these depictions into a usable character reading — how he behaves, how he speaks, what he notices, and how to write him — see [[Samsara Lykor — Personality Profile]]. It is a production document, not a record.
