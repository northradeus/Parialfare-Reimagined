# Leepy



<p class="lead">**Leepy** is the fourth member of the black operations unit [[LENDERMAIRE]] and its sniper. He is recorded as the most psychologically compromised member of the thirteen-member core. His marksmanship originates from hunting rather than military service.</p>

His visible personality is not his own. It was built for him.

## 1. The hunting

Before he was a soldier, Leepy was a hunter. The record establishes the origin of his skill and nothing else about it: no region, no quarry, no family tradition, no name for the place he learned it.

What a hunting origin gives a marksman is not accuracy. It is patience. A hunter waits, sometimes for hours, without moving and without needing the wait to end. He learns distance as a habit rather than a calculation. He learns to kill quietly, from far off, and to walk to the body afterwards and deal with it. The record does not say whether he was taught any of this or arrived at it alone.

It is the one part of him that survived. [[Konish Kimbotin (Reimagined)|Konish Kimbotin]] built the persona, and the persona took the memory, and the shooting was left untouched.

## 2. The persona

Konish trained Leepy to present as lethargic. The record describes the purpose of the construction and not its appearance, so what follows is what the household observes.

He is slow. Not dull, slow — there is a delay between being spoken to and answering, and the delay does not vary with the importance of the question. He is the last to move when a room moves. He dresses himself with his attention somewhere else, and has been recorded wearing his boots on the wrong feet. He does not appear to find this remarkable, and neither does the house.

The persona is not coldness and it is not withdrawal. He is warm, present, and kind, and all of it arrives late. The household has adapted to the lag the way it adapts to everything else: without discussion.

The record does not establish whether the slowness is the block itself or the cost of maintaining it.

## 3. Trauma and conditioning

The record establishes that Leepy killed his own family. It does not record when, how, or in what order, and does not record whether they were found.

Konish trained him to use the lethargic persona as a psychological block, preventing him from fully accessing the memory. The conditioning is intended to keep the trauma suppressed.

*Intended* is the operative word. The record does not establish whether the block is holding, whether it has ever slipped, what the household does when it does, or whether Konish maintains it continuously or it holds on its own. It does not describe the training itself, its duration, or what Konish did to him to make it take.

The record does not describe what Leepy remembers, and does not establish whether he knows that something has been taken from him. A man can be missing a memory and be aware of the shape of the hole.

## 4. The recognition

Leepy is the only member of the household who has been given a persona by someone else, and the only one who lives inside a self he did not build.

The record does not state what this makes him able to see in other people. It notes only that he is recorded as being gentle with new arrivals, and that he has never been recorded asking one of them what they were running from.

## 5. Vanny

He is best friends with [[Vanny]] across every recorded variant. Vanny is the only established social anchor for him in the unit.

The friendship is not explained in the record. It is recorded as holding across continuities, which is more than most facts about him manage.

## 6. Variants

The character exists in two other known continuities, which represent different stages of his development:

*   [[Leepy (Classic)]] — The early version, where he is a "sleep sniper" stereotype.
*   [[Leeper]] — The later version in [[Parialfare: Modern Warfare]], a serious sniper with a chronic sleeping problem carrying the weight of the conditioning.

## Unresolved

- **Trauma depth:** How deep his trauma actually goes, and whether the conditioning is holding.
- **The persona:** Whether the lethargy is the block itself or the effort of maintaining it.
- **Slippage:** Whether the conditioning has ever failed, and what happened when it did.
- **Maintenance:** Whether Konish must maintain the block continuously or whether it holds without him.
- **Awareness:** Whether Leepy knows that something has been taken from him.
- **The training:** What Konish actually did to install the persona, and how long it took.
- **The recognition:** Whether he can see a constructed self in others, and whether he has ever said so.

## Unknowable

- **The reason:** Why he killed his family. This is not recorded and must remain unanswered.
