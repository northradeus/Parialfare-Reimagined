# Wendy



**Wendy** is a sharpshooter of the black operations unit [LENDERMAIRE](/km/factions/lendermaire), recorded in earlier material as "Headshooter." He developed his marksmanship through thousands of hours of airsoft rather than through military service.

## Background (proposed)

*Proposed material. The record holds no background for Wendy beyond his airsoft marksmanship; this section fills that acknowledged blank and is not recovered record.*

He learned to shoot on a competitive airsoft circuit with cash rewards. He left it because he could not stand not knowing whether someone had been paid for him. He had noticed a spectator who stayed too long, a stranger who asked about his scores, and he had begun to suspect that promising players were being reported upward.

He never confirmed it. Leaving put him on the same road anyway, and he ended up inside the household the rumour said he would.

The record does not state whether the circuit had any link to Konish's Group, or whether Konish found him personally. It should not.

## Conduct

His kit is small enough to pack in a minute. His rail is bare. His sling is paracord. He carries a single spare magazine. Pellets from the circuit still turn up in his pockets and in [Joey](/km/characters/lendermaire/joey)'s laundry, and the house tolerates the traces without discussing them.

His name came from the circuit's ranking board. It was the one piece of his old life that came with him, and it is also how his file was easy to find. The house stopped commenting on it, because commenting would mean asking.
