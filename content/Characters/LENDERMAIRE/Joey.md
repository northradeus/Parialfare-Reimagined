# Joey



**Joey** is a background member of the black operations unit [[LENDERMAIRE]], where he manages incoming resources. He is not counted among the group's thirteen core members, and belongs to the additional background personnel who support the unit's operations.

Joey resides with LENDERMAIRE personnel on land owned by [[Konish Kimbotin (Reimagined)]] in the southern [[Ironclad DMZ]]. Background personnel are treated as members of the household's family unit despite their exclusion from the core membership count. He is not established as a combat-deployable operator.

He is also recorded as working for [[Konish's Group]] itself, not only for the household. He answers to Kimbotin directly. The household is not recorded as knowing this.

## The liaison role

Joey manages the flow of resources between Konish's Group and LENDERMAIRE. Supplies, equipment, and matériel move from the coalition into the household through him, and household requirements move back out to the Group through the same channel.

He is not a combatant, not an intelligence asset, and not a decision-maker. He is the person who holds both ledgers. He knows what the Group has, what the house has, and what is missing between them.

His position is placed rather than acquired. The record does not describe how he came to it, and does not establish whether he was chosen for it or simply ended up in it.

## Specialisation

Joey is responsible for logistics and the management of incoming resources. His established responsibilities concern the receipt and management of resources entering LENDERMAIRE's household and organisational structure, and the reporting of household requirements back to Konish's Group. He is the interface between the two.

The exact categories of resources under his responsibility are not recorded.

*Proposed:* Joey maintains an inventory of incoming supplies. Entries include quantities, storage locations, and intended recipients.

*Proposed:* Incoming resources are checked against existing household requirements before being distributed. Damaged, incomplete, or unaccounted-for supplies are recorded separately.

No specific procurement authority is established.

## Resource Management and the Vanny Overlap

A separate unresolved overlap concerns [[Vanny]]'s logistics responsibilities. Vanny is a core member specialising in cyber operations, electronic warfare, and logistics. A note associated with Vanny identifies the question of how Vanny's responsibilities overlap with Joey's resource-management work. The division of responsibility remains unresolved.

*Proposed:* Joey handles structural logistics — the ongoing flow of resources between Konish's Group and the household, including intake, storage, and household-level distribution. Vanny handles operational logistics — the technical requirements of a specific mission, including procurement and coordination. They meet where a mission requirement becomes a supply request.

The division holds at the edges and overlaps in the middle, and neither is recorded as having authority over the other. This is a proposed arrangement and is not established as canon.

## Household Assignment

The household functions as the social unit of LENDERMAIRE. Background personnel are treated as family despite their exclusion from the core membership count.

Joey's domestic responsibilities are not recorded. He is recorded as a member of the household in every social sense: he eats at the table, he is counted as family, and the others treat him as one of their own.

What is not recorded is whether any member of the household knows he reports to Kimbotin.

*Proposed:* Joey maintains shared household supply records. His work includes monitoring consumable stocks and identifying shortages before they affect household operations.

No formal household administrative position is established.

## Operational Classification

Joey's established specialisation is logistics and incoming resources. He is not established as a combat-deployable operator. No field assignment or direct combat responsibility is recorded.

His position within LENDERMAIRE's command structure is not established beyond his background-member classification.
