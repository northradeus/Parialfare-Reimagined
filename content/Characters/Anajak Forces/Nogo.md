# Nogo



**Nogo** is an adult assassin of the [Anajak Forces](/km/factions/anajak-forces), graded A+. Like [Tanishito](/km/characters/anajak-forces/tanishito), he is of mixed Thaiphonese and [Lurkish](/km/locations/countries/lurkinsberg) heritage. He was raised and trained inside a secret facility ([Site-29](/km/locations/site-29)) alongside his assigned partner, Tanishito, and was handpicked by the [Ironish Task Force (ITF)](/factions/ironish-task-force-(itf)) together with her. He was also raised as a spy.

*Not to be confused with Nolgo, a Purgatory character. Nogo is a variant of that name, the two are separate entities, and no facts are shared.*

His recorded description matches Tanishito's and reflects the same mixed Thaiphonese and Lurkish heritage. It is kept in her article and not repeated here.

## 1. Facility record

His childhood and upbringing were controlled by the facility. The record lists no family other than facility personnel.

He holds a final grade of A+. No earlier grades are recorded. The scale, the criteria and the authority that assigns grades are not established.

The record holds no intake record, no training milestones and no personal detail for him.

## 2. Position relative to Tanishito

The facility assigned him as Tanishito's partner, and he was raised alongside her. The two were handpicked by ITF in the same action.

The closeness between them is a tactic. [Ironish Task Force (ITF)](/factions/ironish-task-force-(itf)) knows this and [Northrad](/characters/vista/noradus) does not (see Tanishito's record).

The archives hold no entry on him that is not stated in relation to Tanishito. ITF accounts of Anajak, as recorded, mention Tanishito and not Nogo.

## Unresolved

- **Background:** unestablished beyond his role as Tanishito's partner.
- **Alpha-01 membership:** whether he is in the classified core. Never stated.
- **ITF omission:** why ITF accounts name Tanishito but not him.

## Depiction

The reference draft of Operation Fallow — Episode is the only production piece that shows him beside [Tanishito](/km/characters/anajak-forces/tanishito). It depicts the closeness between them as something he performs perfectly and reads nothing into, and her return to him as a silence he does not notice. The draft is not canon. What it establishes about him is a reading, not a record: that a man trained to know where his partner is standing may not be trained to see what has changed in her.
