# Verdanski

**Verdanski** is an A+ operative of [Anajak Forces](/km/factions/anajak-forces), facility-trained in combat. He is of mixed Cladish and [Thaiphonese](/locations/countries/thaiphon) heritage, an uncommon lineage within a task force whose core membership is predominantly [Lurkish](/km/locations/countries/lurkinsberg).

## 1. Heritage and training

Verdanski is recorded as both Cladish and Thaiphonese, a rare combination in the region. How an individual of partly Cladish descent entered an institutional facility pipeline designed to counter Cladish insurgent formations is not established in the record.

He was trained in combat by a facility and graded **A+**. Which facility trained him—whether [Site-29](/km/locations/site-29) or another installation—is not recorded, nor is his age or prior service.

## 2. Role within Anajak

Although Verdanski holds an A+ combat grade and is registered on the [Anajak Forces](/km/factions/anajak-forces) roster, his specific operational specialty and battlefield role within the task force are not established.
