# Tarnished



<p class="lead">**Tarnished** is the only recorded volunteer in [[Anajak Forces]] who did not arrive through the institution's facility pipeline. He served under the [[Ironish Task Force (ITF)|ITF]] as a special task force leader, then volunteered to join Anajak alongside his own soldiers.</p>

## ITF service

Tarnished led a special task force within the ITF before his transfer. The ITF is a private military contractor and a separate institution from the [[Global Intervention Coalition (GIC)]] that established Anajak — but its personnel also serve inside Anajak, and it handpicked at least two of Anajak's assassins. Tarnished's path is the inverse: he left the contractor's structure to join the force the contractor supplies.

He is explicitly not the ITF commander established as part of [[Alpha-01]]. No link between the two men is established, and the similarity of their positions is the only thing they share.

## The volunteer transfer

Tarnished arrived carrying his own people, which makes his position structurally unlike anyone else on the roster. The facility-raised members were produced by an institution and graded as they went. The leveraged members of the Alpha-01 core were forced in. Tarnished neither was made nor compelled — he brought a unit to a force that already had one.

No grade is recorded for him. Whether a volunteer entering with a retinue is graded on arrival, graded later, or graded at all, is unresolved.

## His soldiers

He volunteered alongside Pich, Kody, Shadow, and many more. The three named are recorded only as his; nothing about their roles, grades or backgrounds is established, and the remainder of the group is unnumbered.

Whether the soldiers volunteered individually or followed Tarnished as his command is not stated. The distinction matters: a following is a faction inside the force, while individual volunteers are simply recruits who arrived together.

## Unresolved

- **Soldiers:** the names and roles of his other soldiers beyond Pich, Kody and Shadow.
- **Grade:** whether a volunteer entering with a retinue is graded on arrival, later, or at all.
- **Motive:** why he left the ITF for Anajak.
- **Rank relative to Ramcher:** his place in Anajak's command structure.
