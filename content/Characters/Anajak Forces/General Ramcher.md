# General Ramcher



**General Ramcher** commands [Anajak Forces](/factions/anajak-forces). The record documents the post he holds rather than the person holding it: nothing about his origin, training, grade or prior service is established, and the office is described entirely by what it does.

## Command functions

The commander of Anajak organises missions, coordinates air strikes, calls reinforcements, and directs where the force will deploy. Taken together these describe a post that sits at the junction of planning, air support and movement — the authority to commit the force, rather than to fight alongside it. To provide the task force's strike aviation, Ramcher transferred the [Thaiphonese](/locations/countries/thaiphon) fighter pilot [Takahashi](/characters/anajak-forces/takahashi) ("Sahara") into Anajak from the inactive [Global Intervention Coalition (GIC)](/factions/global-intervention-coalition-(gic)) roster.

No subordinate commander is named, and no second-in-command is recorded. Whether Ramcher is the sole authority between Anajak's political sponsors and its operators is unresolved.

## Chain of authority

Anajak was established by the [Global Intervention Coalition (GIC)](/factions/global-intervention-coalition-(gic)) and designed by [Northrad](/characters/vista/noradus), who funds it heavily. Ramcher commands the force those institutions created. Whether he answers to the GIC, to Northrad personally, or to both, and where his authority ends and theirs begins, is not established.

He is also not recorded as belonging to any of Anajak's three recruitment pipelines — facility-raised, volunteer, or leveraged. Whether he was graded at all is unknown.
