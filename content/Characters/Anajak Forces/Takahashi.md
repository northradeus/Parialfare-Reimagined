# Takahashi

**Takahashi** (codename **"Sahara"**) is a Thaiphonese fighter pilot serving in [Anajak Forces](factions/anajak-forces). A former professional race car driver who won two championship cups before a crash left him a burn victim, he enlisted in the Thaiphonese Army and was later seconded to the [Global Intervention Coalition (GIC)](factions/global-intervention-coalition-(gic)). Following a period in which the GIC conducted no combat deployments, [General Ramcher](characters/anajak-forces/general-ramcher) personally brought him into Anajak Forces to fly air support for the task force.

## 1. Racing career and the crash

Before entering military service, Takahashi competed as a professional race car driver. He won two championship cups on the circuit. Years of competitive driving conditioned his body to withstand sustained lateral and vertical acceleration, establishing the high G-force tolerance later recorded in his flight evaluations.

His racing career ended in a crash that left him a burn victim. He retired from professional motorsport following the injury and transitioned to military aviation. The record does not name the circuit, the championships won, or the extent of his burn injuries.

## 2. Military service and transfer to Anajak

Takahashi entered the Thaiphonese Army as a fighter pilot after retiring from the track. He was subsequently assigned to the [Global Intervention Coalition (GIC)](factions/global-intervention-coalition-(gic)) following its establishment by [VISTA](factions/vista) on 4 December 2008. Because VISTA's charter contained no provision for funding offensive operations and the twelve delegations authorized no campaign into the [Ironclad DMZ](locations/ironclad-dmz), the GIC conducted no combat deployments during his initial assignment, leaving him grounded on the coalition roster.

[General Ramcher](characters/anajak-forces/general-ramcher), whose command of [Anajak Forces](factions/anajak-forces) includes the coordination of air strikes and air support, transferred Takahashi out of the inactive GIC pool and into Anajak. Within the task force, Takahashi flies the strike and air-support sorties directed from Ramcher's command post.

During post-2008 counter-insurgency operations against [LENDERMAIRE](factions/lendermaire), an airstrike flown by Takahashi killed the LENDERMAIRE shock trooper [Salarean "Salmon"](characters/lendermaire/salmon). The strike makes Takahashi the first Anajak operator confirmed in the record to have killed a member of LENDERMAIRE's thirteen-man core.

## 3. The codename "Sahara"

Inside Anajak Forces, Takahashi operates under the codename **"Sahara"**. The word is [Lurkish](locations/countries/lurkinsberg) and refers to a species of giant sea bird. The designation reflects the linguistic makeup of the task force: although Takahashi is Thaiphonese, the majority of Anajak's core personnel and internal command vocabulary are Lurkish. Who assigned the callsign is not recorded.
