# Hanktonio



**Hanktonio**, also called **Hank** and known by the codename "The Second Hand," is the second-in-command of the umbrella coalition [Konish's Group](factions/konish's-group) and of its black operations unit [LENDERMAIRE](factions/lendermaire). He oversees operations and maintains the coalition's network in [Konish Kimbotin](characters/lendermaire/konish-kimbotin-(reimagined))'s absence, takes the lead when Kimbotin is not around, and is in part responsible for keeping the group intact.

He holds two different posts under one title. In Konish's Group he is second to the head of a network of autonomous militias. In LENDERMAIRE he is second to the head of a household. The record does not distinguish between the two roles, and he is not recorded as finding the distinction difficult.

## 1. The codename

"The Second Hand" is recorded as a codename and not as a description. The record does not establish its origin, who assigned it, or whether he uses it of himself.

Three readings are open and none is confirmed: a hand that works behind the primary; a hand that counts time; a hand that is held in reserve and spent only when the first has failed.

## 2. The temper

Early accounts record Hanktonio as rough, easily angered, and a brawler. Later accounts record him as calm and approachable, and as the household's principal de-escalator during internal disputes.

The record establishes that the change is genuine and not a construction of the kind maintained over other members. It was not installed in him. It grew.

Kimbotin is recorded as having been part of that process. The record does not describe what he did, how long it took, or whether Hanktonio was aware of it while it happened.

This is the only recorded instance of a member of the household changing by his own effort with Kimbotin's assistance rather than by Kimbotin's training. The record does not state whether anyone in the household has noticed this, or what it would mean to them if they had.

## 3. The account

Hanktonio is the sole attributed source for the account of Kimbotin's recruitment method — that Kimbotin was a mysterious man who could not only "fix" people but also find them and persuade them to join.

That account is not independently confirmed. It is the description on which the household's understanding of its own founder substantially rests, and it originates with the man who is second to him.

The record does not establish whether Hanktonio knows that no other account exists. It does not record him claiming to be the only source, and it does not record anyone telling him that he is. He is not recorded as having been asked whether he considers the account complete.

He describes Kimbotin as an enigmatic figure capable of both rehabilitating individuals and tracking them down.

## 4. The wall

Hanktonio is the person to whom the household brings its disputes. He is recorded as the primary de-escalator, as the member who keeps the group intact, and as the one who takes the lead when Kimbotin is absent.

He is also recorded as being held by the same cohesion as the rest of the twelve. He is not exempt from it, and is not recorded as seeking to be.

The record does not establish where Hanktonio takes his own difficulties, whether anyone in the household has ever asked him about them, or whether he has any means of setting down what he carries for others.

## 5. The door

He is recorded as the member who receives new arrivals.

The pattern is consistent: a cup, a gesture toward a chair, an instruction about where to put a bag, and nothing else. He does not explain the household, the unit, or Kimbotin, and does not ask the new arrival what they have come from.

The record does not establish the criteria by which he decides how much welcome to extend, or whether any arrival has ever been received differently from another.

## 6. The seat

He sits nearest the door, with a view of the stairs.

The household reads this as practical. The record does not establish whether it is also a habit of counting, and does not describe him counting anything.

## Unresolved

- **The codename:** its origin, who assigned it, and whether he uses it of himself are not established.
- **The change:** what Kimbotin did, how long it took, and whether Hanktonio was aware of it at the time are not recorded.
- **The account:** whether he knows no other account of Kimbotin's method exists, and whether he has ever been asked whether his own is complete.
- **The burden:** where he takes his own difficulties, and whether anyone has ever asked him about them.
- **The door:** the criteria by which he decides how much welcome to extend to a new arrival.
- **The seat:** whether the position is tactical, habitual, or both.
